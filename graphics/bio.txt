ABOUT (Page description)
---------------------------------
GETGO is a local pabili, padala and errand service for Bongabong, Oriental Mindoro.

Order food and items from stores in town, send parcels and documents, or have a rider run errands for you, like buying medicine or paying bills. A local rider accepts your order, buys it at the counter and brings it to your door.

💗 Food · Shops · Padala · Errands
💸 Pay your rider by GCash, or cash on delivery when your rider offers it
👀 You see the total before you order
🛵 Riders from our own town
