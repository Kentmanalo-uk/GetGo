How GETGO works 👇

1️⃣ Order in the app. Pick a store, or send a padala or errand.
2️⃣ A local rider accepts. Pay by GCash, or cash on delivery when your rider offers it.
3️⃣ Delivered to you. Your rider buys it at the counter and brings it to your door.

You can see your rider's name, rating and where your order is. Unused money comes back to you, and you always see the total before you order.
